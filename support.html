Common questions

How do I open Grown-ups?

Tap the satchel at the top of Home, then Grown-ups. Answer the quick sum.

I've unlocked it, but it's asking again

On the unlock page, tap "I've already unlocked it". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I use it on my iPad too?

Yes. In Grown-ups, turn on "Keep a copy in iCloud" on both devices. Both need the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles the payment, so refunds come from Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I remove everything?

Delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud.
