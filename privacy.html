Privacy

Axy-Axy collects nothing. Here's what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your child's name, goals, photos or voice notes.

Where it's kept

On your device. If you turn on Keep a copy in iCloud, a copy also goes to your family's own private iCloud, run by Apple. Only you can see it. We can't.

Photos, camera and microphone

Only used when you add a photo, a video or a voice note. They stay in the app, and in your iCloud if the copy is on.

The unlock

The unlock is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, your card or what you bought.

Crash reports

If you share analytics with app developers in your device's Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They never include anything about your child.

For children

Axy-Axy is made for children, with a grown-up. Settings, email and the App Store are behind Grown-ups, out of reach of little fingers.

Removing everything

Deleting the app removes everything from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud.

This website

No cookies. No trackers. No sign-up.
